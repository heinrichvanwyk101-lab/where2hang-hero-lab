Saadiyat 12 built

The Cultural District is now one composition: five museums on their real sites, the fabric between them white and stepped.

  • Built
    Louvre Abu Dhabi

    An open two-layer lattice dome over white galleries on a turquoise platform in the sea, pool frames and a causeway. Light comes through the cells at night.

  • Built
    Zayed National Museum

    Five curved silver-lattice sails bowing over a lagoon on a white faceted podium, the tallest 123 m, glowing through the cells at night.

  • Built
    Manarat Al Saadiyat

    The low white gallery with its deep overhanging roof and dark glass base, on the surveyed footprint.

  • Built
    Berklee Abu Dhabi

    The white cube with the glass drum, next to Manarat.

  • Built
    Natural History Museum

    Stepped white cubes with planted terraces and glazed bands, on the channel by the bridge landing.

  • Built
    Guggenheim Abu Dhabi

    A leaning heap of white slabs and cones on its platform at the tip, honeycomb cells along the water.

  • Built
    teamLab Phenomena

    Two merged white shells 150 m across with openings cut in, east of the Natural History Museum on the same shore.

  • Built
    Park Hyatt, Jumeirah Saadiyat

    Low white resort wings with terracotta roofs round a pool court, on the two big beach parcels the survey carries. Names are the best reading of the beach's order.

  • Built
    St. Regis Saadiyat

    Cream palace-hotel with a dome and two terracotta-roofed arms stepping down to the beach, pool, beach walk and palms. Its parcel is not in the survey, so the site was grid-searched against the outline and roads, west of Park Hyatt.

  • Built
    Mamsha Al Saadiyat

    The full 1.4 km beachfront: nine white terraced blocks laid along the surveyed shore, each stepping down to the beach with a glass band per floor and a pool, restaurant terraces under canopies, the promenade with its palm row, umbrellas on the sand.

  • Built
    Saadiyat Grove

    Built as complete: a grid of white terraced blocks with planted roofs, turned with the Zayed National Museum and filling the district round it, skipping the museum zones and the lagoon.

  • Built
    NYU Abu Dhabi

    Nine pale campus blocks with dark glass bands round a quad, with the taller library block, on the survey's own records.

Against your priority list. Everything on it is built. The register now also carries the districts built as complete rather than as survey fabric: Saadiyat Grove, Mamsha's full beachfront, East Yas as villas, and Yas Bay with Pier71 and Café del Mar.

Two placements are from published coordinates rather than the survey: the Louvre (its platform is in the water off the drawn coast) and the Guggenheim. The Natural History Museum, teamLab, St. Regis and the Grove's grid sit on spots found against the road and shoreline data. Al Seef Village is on the one big low record on the creek, which is the best reading of the survey.
